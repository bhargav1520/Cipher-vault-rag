Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager
approval. Core collaboration hours are 10 AM to 4 PM local time, during
which employees are expected to be reachable on Slack and available for
meetings. Equipment requests for home office setups, including monitors
and ergonomic chairs, can be submitted through the internal IT portal and
are typically approved within five business days.

Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid leave per year, credited
monthly. Unused leave up to 10 days may be carried over into the next
calendar year. Sick leave is tracked separately and does not count
against the annual leave balance. Leave requests should be submitted at
least two weeks in advance except in cases of medical emergency.
