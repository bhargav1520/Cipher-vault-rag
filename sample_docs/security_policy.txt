Data Security Policy

Access Control
All employee access to internal systems follows the principle of least
privilege: employees are granted only the permissions necessary for
their role. Access requests beyond default role permissions require
manager approval and are logged. Access reviews are conducted
quarterly, and any unused permissions are automatically revoked after
90 days of inactivity.

Password Requirements
All accounts must use passwords of at least 12 characters, combining
uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Multi-factor authentication
is mandatory for all systems containing customer data. Passwords must
be rotated every 180 days and cannot repeat any of the previous five
passwords used.

Data Classification
Data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and
Restricted. Restricted data, including customer payment information
and government IDs, must be encrypted both at rest and in transit, and
access is limited to a named list of approved personnel. Confidential
data must be encrypted at rest but may be shared internally without
additional approval.

Incident Response
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security team
within one hour of discovery. The security team will triage the
incident within four hours and determine whether customer
notification is required under applicable regulations. A full incident
report, including root cause and remediation steps, must be completed
within five business days of resolution.

Third-Party Vendors
All third-party vendors with access to company data must sign a data
processing agreement and undergo a security review before onboarding.
Vendor access is reviewed annually, and any vendor found non-compliant
with this policy has access revoked within 24 hours.
